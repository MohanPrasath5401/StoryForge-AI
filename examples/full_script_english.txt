# The Rainy Market  (English demo, ~1 minute)
@setting: a cozy rainy street market at dusk, string lights, puddles reflecting warm lanterns
@music: warm acoustic guitar, gentle piano, rainy evening, heartwarming
@motion: soft rain falling, steam rising from food stalls, slow camera push-in

[06,17] Character 06 had run his little noodle stall for forty years, and every evening the rain made the market glow.
Today a schoolgirl stood shivering beside his stall, clutching her backpack and staring at the steaming bowls.
He smiled, wiped his hands on his apron, and slid a warm bowl across the counter without a word.
"This one is on the house," he said, "but you must promise to tell me about your day."

@setting: under a red umbrella beside the noodle stall
[06,17] Between sips of soup, Character 17 told him about her spelling test and the friend who had moved away.
The old chef listened carefully, nodding, the way only someone who has lived a long life can listen.

@setting: the market at night, rain easing, golden lights
@music: hopeful, soft strings, gentle ending
[06,17] When the rain finally stopped, she hugged him goodbye and promised to come back tomorrow.
And that is how a bowl of noodles became the start of a beautiful friendship.
